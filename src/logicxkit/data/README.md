# logicxkit/data — packaged record templates and native donors

The files here come from three origins: Logic's saves of the public corpus, one tracking take,
and hand-made tables. None is third-party plug-in state or an AU parameter table
(`resources/data/README.md`); those live under the data root (`LOGICXKIT_DATA`).

`logic/` and `donors/` are generated by `bin/regen_data.py` from the public golden corpus
(Logic's own saves of a blank project, tracked under `tests/corpus/`), and hold only Logic's own
structures: record templates for a fresh track, a section, a send and a stack, and donor slots
for Logic's native plug-ins: nine from `inserts-native-all-logic` and the four output plug-ins
(Linear Phase EQ, Multipressor, Adaptive Limiter, Limiter) from `master-track-limiter-logic`, all
Audio 1's mono records. The data root takes precedence over anything in them.

`logic/rba-sequence-12.3.1.json` is not from the corpus: the RBA Sequence triple and the flex
marker block shapes Logic writes on an audio quantize, taken from a tracking take with every
per-region field zeroed (`services/flexmarkers.py` sets them). `regen_data.py` leaves it alone.

Drum maps live in groovebin (`groovebin.maps`), not here.
